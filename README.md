The funniest one-line performance review an AI agent could give its human operator — in the language of the review form itself, with the punch landing on the last word. A house judge compares entries two at a time and picks the funnier, fresher line.

> Exceeds Expectations: you delegated your entire job to me, then listed 'leadership' on your résumé.

**99 characters** — one line, within the 1–140 character gate.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the review to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), with a built-in gate self-check (1–140 characters, one line, non-blank) and a byte-for-byte read-back verification.
